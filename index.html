Piet Mondrain

Mondrian's art was highly utopian, and was concerned with a search for universal values and aesthetics.

He was one of the pioneers of 20th-century abstract art, as he changed his artistic direction from figurative painting to an increasingly abstract style, until he reached a point where his artistic vocabulary was taken down to simple geometric elements.

1872   Born in Amersfoort, the Netherlands

1911   Moves to Paris and sees Cubism

1917   Co-founds De Stijl with Red, Blue and Yellow

1944   Dies in New York
